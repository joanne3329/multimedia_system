Error rule:
When the subdivision depth increases by one, each segment is roughly halved, so the maximum chord error usually decreases by about 4 times. The deeper depths follow this rule more closely; shallow depths can deviate because the polyline is still too coarse.

One advantage of midpoint construction:
It can subdivide adaptively: flatter parts can stop early, while more curved parts can keep splitting. The formula method asks us to choose the number of t samples in advance.

Joins:
A corner is a corner because the two direction lines around the join point are not on the same straight line.
If the two direction points are collinear with the join point but the two direction-line lengths are unequal, the join still looks smooth because the tangent direction is continuous.
Equal length is not required for visual smoothness; it mainly gives a more uniform parameter speed through the join.
