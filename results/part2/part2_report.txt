Part 2: Inside and Outside

Sign convention:
In image coordinates, y increases downward. A downward edge adds +1 and an upward edge adds -1.
The scanline is evaluated at each pixel center. A span is filled whenever the running winding number is non-zero.

Part 2.2 - five-pointed star:
Winding number at the center: -2
The center is filled because its winding number is non-zero.
This differs from filling alternate spans with the even-odd rule.

Part 2.3 - two circle subpaths:
Center winding number when both circles have the same direction: -2
Center winding number when the inner circle is reversed: 0
When both circles have the same direction, their winding contributions add, so the center is filled.
When the inner circle is reversed, its contribution cancels the outer circle, so the center becomes a hole.
No point is moved. Only the visiting order of the inner-circle points is reversed.
Therefore, a font must store the inner and outer contours of the letter O in opposite directions so that the non-zero winding rule leaves the center unfilled.
